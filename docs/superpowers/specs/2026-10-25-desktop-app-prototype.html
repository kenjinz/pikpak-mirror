    PikPak Backup

    Back up this PC to PikPak

    Pick the folders you care about. They're copied to your PikPak every evening, and you can get them back from any PC.

    • Runs by itselfEvery evening while your PC is on. You don't need to open this app.
    • Deleted something by mistake?Anything you delete or overwrite is kept for 30 days.
    • Private folders stay privateFolders you mark private are locked before they leave your PC.

    Step 1 of 4

    Sign in to PikPak

    Use the same email and password as the PikPak app on your phone.

    Your password goes only to PikPak. It's kept on this PC so backups can run while you're away.

    Step 2 of 4

    Choose what to back up

    We ticked the folders that fit in your PikPak. Untick anything you don't need.

    Free PikPak account, 6 GB

    Mark a folder Private to lock it with a recovery key before it's uploaded. Only this app, with that key, can open it.

    Step 3 of 4

    Save your recovery key

    Your private folders are locked with this key before they leave your PC. If this PC ever breaks, you'll need it to open them on a new one. Nobody else has a copy, not even PikPak.

    7HQX2 MPD4T W9KZR C3VNE 6BJYA FU8LS
    No zeros or O's, no ones or L's, so it's easy to copy by hand.

    You can see it again any time in Settings.

    Step 4 of 4

    You're all set

    Your backup also shows up in File Explorer as drive P:, so you can open files from PikPak like any other folder.
