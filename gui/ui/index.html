Keep your folders in sync with PikPak

Pick the folders you care about. Changes you make on this PC or in PikPak are copied to the other side every hour.

Step 3 of 4

Save your recovery key

Your encrypted folders are locked with this key before they leave your PC. If this PC ever breaks, you'll need it to open them on a new one. Nobody else has a copy, not even PikPak.

No zeros or O's, no ones or L's, so it's easy to copy by hand.

You can see it again any time in Settings.
